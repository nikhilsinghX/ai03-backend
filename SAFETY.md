# Safety and responsible use

- Use fictional candidate data during demos and development.
- Tell candidates they are interacting with an AI system and require consent before starting.
- Assess only job-related skills and evidence from the approved rubric. Do not infer or score protected characteristics, appearance, accent, personality, honesty, or culture fit.
- The report is a reviewer aid, not an automatic hire/reject decision. A human reviewer remains responsible.
- Speech recognition can be inaccurate. Show recognized text and let candidates correct or type their answer.
- Treat mock-mode assessment as test data only.
